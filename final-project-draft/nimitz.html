Vessel 2: USS Nimitz (CVN-68)

Brief Service History

The USS Nimitz is a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier commissioned in 1975. It has been a key asset in U.S. naval operations, participating in various military engagements and humanitarian missions around the world.
