Overview

The U.S. Navy has a rich history of vessels that have played crucial roles in national defense and global maritime operations. This project explores the evolution of naval ships, highlighting key vessels that have shaped the Navy's legacy.

Vessel 1: USS Enterprise (CVN-65)

Brief Service History

The USS Enterprise was the world's first nuclear-powered aircraft carrier, commissioned in 1961. It served for over 50 years, participating in numerous conflicts and humanitarian missions before being decommissioned in 2017.

Vessel 2: USS Nimitz (CVN-68)

Brief Service History

The USS Nimitz is a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier of the United States Navy. Commissioned in 1975, it is the second of the Nimitz-class carriers and has been a cornerstone of the Navy's power projection capabilities.
